Understanding
Protein.

Learn what protein does, why it matters and how balanced meals can help support an active lifestyle.

THE FUNDAMENTAL

Protein is
more than muscle.

Protein is an important nutrient that your body uses for many different functions. It helps support muscles, tissues and normal body processes.

For people who exercise, getting protein regularly as part of a balanced diet can support training and recovery.

01 · UNDERSTAND IT

What Is Protein?

Protein is a nutrient made from smaller building blocks called amino acids.

01

Muscle Support

Protein provides amino acids that the body uses to maintain and repair muscle tissue.

02

Body Tissues

Protein is involved in maintaining many tissues throughout the body.

03

Body Functions

Proteins are also involved in many normal processes that keep the body functioning.

04

Daily Nutrition

Protein is one part of a balanced diet alongside carbohydrates, fats, vitamins, minerals and water.

02 · WHY IT MATTERS

Why Does Protein Matter?

Protein plays an important role in everyday nutrition, especially when combined with regular physical activity.

01

Supports Muscle Maintenance

Your muscles are constantly being maintained. Protein provides amino acids that contribute to normal muscle maintenance.

02

Supports Training Recovery

After exercise, your body needs adequate nutrition and recovery. Protein is one part of that overall process.

03

Helps Build Balanced Meals

Including a protein source in meals can make it easier to build varied and balanced meals.

04

Works With Other Nutrients

Protein does not work alone. Carbohydrates, healthy fats, vitamins, minerals and fluids also have important roles.

03 · FOOD SOURCES

Where Can You Get Protein?

Protein can come from many different foods. Variety can help you build a balanced diet.

01

Eggs

Eggs are a convenient protein-containing food that can fit into many meals.

02

Milk

Milk provides protein along with other nutrients such as calcium.

03

Dal & Legumes

Dal, beans and other legumes can contribute plant-based protein to meals.

04

Chickpeas

Chickpeas can be used in meals and snacks as a source of plant-based protein.

05

Peanuts

Peanuts contain protein as well as fats and can be included as part of a varied diet.

06

Paneer & Soy

Foods such as paneer and soy products can also provide protein.

04 · BUILD A MEAL

Protein
Meets Balance.

A balanced meal does not need to revolve around protein alone.

A simple meal can combine a protein source with carbohydrates, vegetables or fruit, healthy fats and enough fluids.

The exact combination can change depending on your preferences, routine and available foods.

05 · DAILY HABITS

Think About The Whole Day.

Nutrition is not decided by one meal. Your overall eating pattern matters.

01

Breakfast

Include a protein-containing food alongside other foods that give you energy and nutrients.

02

Lunch

Build a varied meal with a protein source, carbohydrates and vegetables or other plant foods.

03

After Training

A normal balanced meal or snack containing protein can be part of your post-training routine.

04

Dinner

Keep your evening meal varied rather than focusing on one nutrient alone.

06 · AVOID THESE

Common Mistakes.

01

Focusing Only On Protein

Protein is important, but your body also needs carbohydrates, fats, vitamins, minerals and fluids.

02

Ignoring Food Variety

Eating a wide variety of foods can help provide different nutrients.

03

Chasing Extreme Diets

Healthy nutrition does not require crash diets, extreme restriction or cutting out entire food groups without a medical reason.

04

Forgetting Recovery

Food is only one part of fitness. Sleep, rest and sensible training also matter.

07 · IMPORTANT

More Protein Isn't Automatically Better.

Protein is an important nutrient, but increasing one nutrient without considering the rest of your diet is not the goal.

01

Balance

Think about your overall diet rather than focusing on one nutrient.

02

Variety

Different foods provide different nutrients, so variety matters.

03

Consistency

Regular balanced meals are more useful than constantly changing your diet.

04

Recovery

Nutrition works alongside sleep, rest and appropriate physical activity.
