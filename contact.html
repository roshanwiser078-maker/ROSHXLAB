Here are a few common questions about ROSHXLAB.

Is ROSHXLAB a personal training service?

No. ROSHXLAB is an educational fitness platform providing general information about training, nutrition, recovery and habits.

Can ROSHXLAB create a personal workout plan?

ROSHXLAB content focuses on general guidance. Individual training needs can vary, especially with injuries, medical conditions or other personal considerations.

Can I suggest a topic?

Yes. Content suggestions are welcome. You can use the contact form to share an article, workout or fitness topic you'd like to see covered.

Will the contact form send messages immediately?

The current form is a visual front-end form. A backend or form service will need to be connected before messages are actually delivered.
