MUSCLE GROWTH FUNDAMENTALS

Train.
Recover.
Progress.

Building muscle takes consistency. Focus on good technique, appropriate resistance, enough food and adequate recovery.

Progressive Overload

As an exercise becomes easier, gradually increase the challenge through additional repetitions, manageable resistance, improved technique or a harder variation.

MUSCLE BUILDING PRINCIPLES

The Process
Matters.

Training is only one part of building muscle. Consistency, nutrition and recovery all matter.

01 · TECHNIQUE

Control Your Reps

Learn the movement and use a controlled range instead of rushing repetitions.

02 · PROGRESSION

Progress Gradually

When a movement becomes easier, gradually increase the challenge.

03 · RECOVERY

Recover Properly

Give your body enough time to recover between demanding training sessions.
