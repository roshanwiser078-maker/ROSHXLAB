BEFORE YOU START

Keep It
Simple.

You don't need complicated workouts when you're starting. Focus on learning the movement, staying consistent and gradually improving.

Your First Goal

Don't worry about lifting heavy or doing advanced exercises. Build a solid movement foundation first. If an exercise causes pain, stop and seek guidance from a qualified adult or fitness professional.

02 · BEGINNER ROUTINE

Your First
Workout.

You don't need all 15 exercises in one session. Start with a small selection and focus on learning each movement.

STEP 01

Warm Up

5–10 minutes

Start with easy movement such as marching in place and gentle mobility.

STEP 02

Main Exercises

4–6 exercises

Choose basic movements covering different areas of the body.

STEP 03

Recovery

After training

Finish calmly and give your body enough time to recover before the next demanding session.

START SMART

Learn First.
Progress Later.

Beginners don't need to rush into advanced training. Build your movement skills first.

01 · TECHNIQUE

Learn The Movement

Practice slowly and focus on comfortable, controlled movement.

02 · CONSISTENCY

Keep Showing Up

A simple routine you can consistently follow is more useful than a complicated routine.

03 · PROGRESSION

Increase Gradually

When the current level becomes comfortable, gradually make the exercise more challenging.
