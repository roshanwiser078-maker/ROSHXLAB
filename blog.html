FITNESS MYTHS

Question The Hype.

Fitness information is full of shortcuts and exaggerated claims. Start with the basics.

MYTH 01

You Can Spot-Reduce Fat

Training a particular muscle does not selectively remove body fat from that area.

MYTH 02

More Training Is Always Better

Training needs to be balanced with recovery. More isn't automatically better.

MYTH 03

You Need Expensive Equipment

Effective exercise can be built around bodyweight movements and simple equipment.

MYTH 04

One Food Changes Everything

Long-term nutrition is about overall eating patterns, not a single magic food.
