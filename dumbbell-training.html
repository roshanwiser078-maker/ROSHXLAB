01 · DUMBBELL BASICS

Master The
Basics.

Good dumbbell training starts with control, comfortable resistance and consistent technique.

01 · WEIGHT

Choose Manageable Weight

Start with a resistance that allows you to perform the movement with good control.

02 · CONTROL

Control Every Rep

Avoid rushing. Use a controlled movement through a comfortable range.

03 · PROGRESSION

Progress Gradually

Improve your technique first, then gradually increase repetitions or resistance.

Technique Comes First

A heavier dumbbell is not automatically better. Choose a manageable load and focus on performing each repetition with control. If you cannot maintain good technique, reduce the difficulty.

05 · PROGRESSION

Make Training
Harder Slowly.

Progress doesn't always mean immediately picking up heavier dumbbells.

01

Improve Technique

Make each repetition controlled and consistent before increasing difficulty.

02

Add Repetitions

When an exercise becomes comfortable, gradually work toward the upper end of your target range.

03

Increase Resistance

Once your current resistance feels manageable, a small increase can provide a new challenge.

TRAIN SMART

Train.
Recover.
Repeat.

Consistent training works best when it is balanced with recovery.

Keep Your Training Sustainable

Use manageable resistance, prioritize good form, take rest days and avoid trying to progress too quickly. If an exercise causes pain, stop and seek guidance from a qualified adult or fitness professional.
