01 · THE BASICS

What Actually
Helps?

There is no exercise that selectively removes fat from only one area. Overall body-fat changes come from your wider activity, eating habits, growth and lifestyle.

For a broader training foundation, explore our home workouts guide and strength training guide .

Sleep & Recovery

No Spot Reduction

Crunches, planks and other core exercises can strengthen your abdominal muscles, but they cannot specifically choose where your body loses fat. Genetics and overall body composition influence where fat is stored and lost.
