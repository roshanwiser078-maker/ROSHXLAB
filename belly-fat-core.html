01 · THE BASICS

What Actually
Helps?

There is no exercise that selectively removes fat from only one area. Overall body-fat changes come from your wider activity, eating habits, growth and lifestyle.

01 · ACTIVITY

Move Regularly

Walking, sports, cycling and other enjoyable physical activities all contribute to an active lifestyle.

02 · STRENGTH

Strength Training

Full-body strength training helps develop strength, movement skills and fitness.

03 · NUTRITION

Balanced Eating

Focus on regular balanced meals rather than extreme dieting or cutting out entire food groups.

04 · RECOVERY

Sleep & Recovery

Your body needs adequate sleep and recovery to support health, learning and physical activity.

No Spot Reduction

Crunches, planks and other core exercises can strengthen your abdominal muscles, but they cannot specifically choose where your body loses fat. Genetics and overall body composition influence where fat is stored and lost.

03 · SIMPLE APPROACH

Think
Whole Body.

Core training works best as part of a broader fitness routine rather than as the only training you do.

TRAINING

Full-Body Strength

Include exercises for your legs, pushing muscles, pulling muscles and core.

MOVEMENT

Stay Active

Add walking, sports, cycling or another activity you genuinely enjoy.

RECOVERY

Give Yourself Time

Recovery is part of training. Don't try to compensate by exercising excessively.

04 · AVOID THE HYPE

Skip The
Shortcuts.

Fitness should be built around sustainable habits, not extreme promises.

01 · NO CRASH DIETS

Don't Starve Yourself

Extreme food restriction is not a healthy shortcut, especially while you're still growing.

02 · NO OVERTRAINING

More Isn't Always Better

Hundreds of repetitions or exhausting daily workouts are not required for a strong core.

03 · NO MAGIC EXERCISE

No Spot-Fat Exercise

No single exercise can tell your body exactly where to lose fat.
